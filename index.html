Week 7

Tyler Grosskopf was 28th of 29 two weeks ago. Last week she went 4th. This week Makai Lemon held off Pickens and Lamb, and she held off the rest of the league by 18.40 for her first win. Four Tylers in this league, and she's the first one with a W.

🥇 Tyler Grosskopf, 150.96 pts, $56.00 🥈 Brian Smith, 132.56 pts, $28.00 🥉 Traci Phelps, 130.02 pts, $14.00 4th: Blaze Swift, 128.86 pts, $7.00 · 5th: Skylar Hays, 128.32 pts, $7.00

Brian Smith has taken the last paying spot twice this year, and this week he's runner-up, his best finish of the season. Traci, the only other rookie with a win, took 3rd, and Blaze Swift has now cashed three straight (6th, 3rd, 4th). Last week's winner Robert Bridges went 27th of 28, so this week, nobody is paying Rob.

Under the radar: Cody Alesandrini took the last paying spot at 123.00. Tyler Ridings, last week's runner-up, finished 11th at 122.36. That's 0.64 between $5.60 and nothing, and five of seven weeks now the 10th/11th line has come down to less than a point. Daniel Goldberg went 6th for his first check of the year, and Eli Jackel (first man out twice) finally landed on the right side of it in 7th. Scott Phelps (23rd) is now the only member who's played all seven weeks without a dollar to show for it.

Power Rankings shuffle: Tyler Grosskopf is the new #1, 13th to 1st, and nobody had climbed more than three spots to take the top spot before. Jared Berquist slides to 2nd after an 18th, Blaze Swift climbs to 3rd, and Brian Smith jumps 12th to 6th. Skylar Hays (28th to 19th) and Eli Jackel (18th to 11th) are the week's other big movers. Going the other way, Robert Bridges drops 5 to 7th, Cody falls 4 to 8th, and Marcus Bailey (14th) and Peyton Hale (29th) slide 5 each. Week 2 aged out of the five-week window and took the commissioner's runner-up with it: he cashed in 8th and still dropped 4 to 9th.

Accountability Corner: 28 of 34 played, up from 25. Edris Ibrahim and Tyler Brock are now 0 for 7. Sean Amedick has missed five straight, Cameron Aud three straight, and Matt Meres two straight since his Week 5 runner-up. Josh McLaughlin sat out for the first time after playing the first six. $5 refund each. Justin Lindstrom did show up and finished 28th of 28 at 79.16.

Season money: Cody Alesandrini $138.40 · Jared Berquist $80.25 · Traci Phelps $77.05 (Tyler Grosskopf jumps from $12.05 to $68.05, $1.20 behind Brandon Ge for 4th)

High score of the week: Tyler Grosskopf, 150.96. It's the lowest winning score of the season for the second straight week (Robert's 161.34 was the previous low), and nobody else got within 18 points of it.
